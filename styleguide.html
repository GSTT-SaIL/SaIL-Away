Surfaces and shadows

White surfaces, told apart by shadow. Grey (--group) is for panels holding white cards.

Banner and board

The board's rounded top casts a soft shadow up onto the white banner.

Cards

On grey: --line-strong border + --shadow-card
On white: no border, --shadow-raised
Pop-ups and dialogs: --shadow-pop
